I bought No Ads but ads still show.

Sign in with the account you bought with, then Settings › Restore Purchases.

How does the tide clock work?

When the clock turns green, shallow passages open. Moon's Howl freezes it.

I have trouble telling the colors apart.

Every color has its own symbol on the boats and bandanas.

Does No Ads remove every ad?

It removes ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay.

I reinstalled the game and lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device and is deleted with the game. Purchases can be restored in Settings.

Why does the game need internet?

Ads, the store and purchases need a connection.

How do I change the language?

In the game: Settings › Language.

How do I manage ad privacy choices?

In the game: Settings › Privacy Options.

I found a bug or a level I can't pass.

Write to us with the level number and your device.
