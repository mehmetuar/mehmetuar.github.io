Features

A boat jam puzzle where the tide changes the rules

Tap a boat with a clear path to sail it to the dock, and the crew waiting on the pier hops aboard the boat of their color. Dock space is limited, and the water keeps rising and falling.

🌊

The tide decides

Shallow passages only open at high water. A boat that is stuck now may sail free a few seconds later. Time your moves and plan your order.

🗺️

Chapters to explore

Sail through Dawn Cove, Kraken Strait, Lighthouse Isle and more, following the Logbook story to the Great Tide Treasure.

🧊

Summer & Glacier worlds

From a sunny summer harbor to the icy Glacier world with frozen boats that wait for the ice to melt and icebergs that block the way.

🐙

Tricky obstacles

The Kraken grabs boats, grounded boats sit in the way, docks get narrow and storm levels race the clock.

👁️

Color-blind friendly

Every color has its own symbol, so every player can tell the boats and crew apart at a glance.

⚡

Quick levels

Easy to learn, satisfying to master. Short levels you can play anywhere, in 14 languages.

FAQ
I bought No Ads but interstitial ads still show. What should I do?

Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store / Google account you used for the purchase, then open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. If ads still appear, email us with your receipt.

Does No Ads remove every ad?

No Ads removes the interstitial ads between levels. Optional rewarded videos (for bonus coins and boosters) stay available, because you choose to watch them.

I reinstalled the game and lost my progress.

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the game deletes its save. Purchases can always be restored from Settings › Restore Purchases.

Why does the game need an internet connection?

Captain Match is free to play and funded by ads, and the store, rewarded videos and purchases all need a connection. Please check Wi‑Fi or mobile data if the game will not load.

How does the tide clock work?

The clock at the top right shows the tide cycle. When it turns green the water is high and shallow passages open. Howl freezes the clock so an open channel stays open.

I have trouble telling the colors apart.

Every color has its own symbol on the boats and on the crew's bandanas, so you can match by symbol instead of color.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the game and pick a language. Captain Match is available in 14 languages.

How do I manage ad privacy choices?

Open Settings › Privacy Options in the game to review or change your consent at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

I found a bug or a level that cannot be solved.

Email us with the level number, your device and, if possible, a screenshot. Every level is tested to be solvable, but we love a good bug report.
